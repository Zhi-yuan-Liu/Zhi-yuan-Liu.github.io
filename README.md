I am an undergraduate student in Mathematics at the **Southern University of Science and Technology (SUSTech)**. My advisor is [Ziqing Xiang](http://ziqing.org/).

My current interests include combinatorics, incidence geometry, discrete geometry, and related topics. I use this page to share my research, presentations, and notes on mathematics.
